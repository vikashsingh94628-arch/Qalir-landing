What Qalir does

  • Remembers. Every user gets their own memory. Your preferences, past conversations and ongoing work carry over - no starting from scratch.
  • Connects to your apps. Link apps like your Gmail. Qalir can read and work in them, and always asks for your yes before sending.
  • Searches the web. Fresh answers from the internet when it matters, not stale guesses.
  • Hands off payments. When it's time to pay, you get the merchant or UPI link and pay directly. Money never passes through Qalir.
  • Stays open, yet safe. Anyone who messages gets a reply, with a limited mode for new users. Your things stay yours alone.

Privacy and control, by design

Secrets are never typed into chat. Passwords go through a one-time secure link - valid for 15 minutes - into an encrypted vault, and are used only for your work, on your instruction.

Everything Qalir does follows one rule: your permission first, then action.

Frequently asked questions

What is Qalir?

A personal AI assistant that runs on Telegram. It remembers your context, searches the web, connects to your apps, and gets tasks done for you.

Who built Qalir?

Qalir is built by Vikash Singh.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Qalir works on Telegram, which you likely already have. There is no new app, and no signup beyond the invite login on this page.

How do I get access?

Qalir is invite-only right now. Request an invite with your email; once approved, log in on this page and you'll be redirected to the Telegram bot, already linked to your account.

Are my passwords and data safe?

Secrets are never shared in chat - only through one-time secure links that expire in 15 minutes, into an encrypted vault. Qalir always asks before sending anything on your behalf.

Does Qalir make payments for me?

No. When something needs paying, Qalir hands you the merchant or UPI link and you pay directly. Money never passes through Qalir.
